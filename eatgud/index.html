Eatgud.

Eatgud names the foods in a photo of your meal, on your iPhone, and works out calories and macros from a table inside the app. You check each portion before anything is logged. It is not a diet app.

What it does

Photo to plate

Apple's on-device model names the foods. Without Apple Intelligence, add them by hand.

You stay in charge

Swap a food, change a portion, or add one. Nothing is logged until you say so.

Numbers from a table

Approximate values for 175 common foods, stored in the app. Not from the photo and not from the internet.

It learns your portions

Correct a large bowl of rice once and the next one starts where you left it.

Today, Meals, Trends

Totals, a week at a glance, and 7 and 30 day averages. Daily targets are optional and nothing is graded.

Your data

No account, no ads, no tracking and no network. Your meals and photos stay on your phone, and the photo is read on the phone. It does not use Apple Health. The full version is in the privacy policy.
