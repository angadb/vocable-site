Support

Help and contact. Last updated 25 October 2026. Eatgud has not been released yet.

The quickest way to get help is to write to GudApps@icloud.com. I read everything and answer it myself.

Common questions

The photo was not recognised.
Eatgud names foods with Apple's on-device model, which needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on. Without it, or if it gets nothing from the photo, add the foods by hand. Naming the first photo can take up to a minute.

The portion is wrong.
Portions from a photo are guesses. Change any food or portion on the "Check the plate" screen before you log it. Eatgud remembers your correction for next time.

Where do the calories come from?
From a table of common foods stored inside the app, with approximate values. They do not come from the photo or the internet. Treat them as estimates.

I scanned a barcode and nothing came up.
Eatgud recognises that a photo has a barcode, but it has no product list yet. It opens "Add by hand" instead.

Does it keep my photos?
A small copy of each, on your iPhone only, unless you turn off "Keep photos" in Settings. Nothing goes to your Photos library.

Does it connect to Apple Health?
No.

Will my meals move to a new iPhone?
Only if the new phone is restored from a backup of the old one. Meals are kept on your iPhone and nowhere else, because Eatgud has no account, no sync and no export.

How do I delete my data?
Use "Erase everything" in Settings, or delete the app.

Is this dietary advice?
No. Eatgud is a log. If you have a health condition or an eating disorder, talk to a doctor or dietitian.
