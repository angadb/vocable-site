Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026. Rungud has not been released yet.

Rungud ("the App") is developed by Angad Singh Bedi ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

The short version: I don't collect your data. The App reads your runs from Apple Health on your device, and nothing leaves your device unless you export a file yourself.

Apple Health

With your permission, the App reads the following from Apple Health:

  • Running workouts
  • VO2 max
  • Resting heart rate
  • Sleep

The App only reads this data. It never writes to Apple Health. Health data is used on your device to show your runs and to build and adjust your training plan. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone.

You can change or remove the App's access at any time in the Health app, under Sharing, or in Settings.

Data That Stays on Your Device

  • Your training plan and the answers you gave when setting it up
  • Your app settings

The App does not use iCloud sync. Deleting the App deletes this data. It does not delete anything from Apple Health.

Your Numbers and Feedback

The App keeps a few plain counts of how you use it, on your iPhone only: the date you first opened it, how many days you opened it, how many plans you made or removed, how many workouts you opened, how many times you copied or shared an export or opened the method page and the widget guide, and whether you are reading Apple Health and have a plan (yes or no only). These counts hold nothing read from Apple Health, such as distances, paces, heart rate or sleep, and the App has no way to send them.

If you choose "Send feedback", the App opens an email to me in your own mail app. If you leave "Include in feedback emails" on, the counts are added to that email, where you can read, change or delete them before you send it. If you send it, I receive your email address and whatever the email contains, and I use them only to read and answer your feedback.

Exports

You can export your runs as CSV or JSON, or as a text summary written to be pasted into an AI assistant. Exports are created on your device and go only where you choose to send them. If you paste a summary into another service, that service's own privacy policy applies to it.

Network Requests, Analytics and Tracking

The App makes no network requests. The training plan is worked out on your device from fixed rules: there is no AI model and no server. The App contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Apple gives app developers anonymous, combined statistics from people who have chosen to share them: how many people installed, opened or deleted the App, and crash reports. I can see those totals. I cannot see a person or a device. You control this on your iPhone in Settings, under Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements, Share With App Developers.

Not Medical Advice

The App is a training aid, not a medical device. Talk to a doctor before starting a training plan if you have any health concerns.

Children

The App collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and its date.
