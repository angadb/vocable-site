What it does

Reads, never writes

Your running workouts come from Apple Health. Rungud only reads them.

A plan from rules

Phases, weekly distance and paces worked out on the phone. No AI and no server.

It adjusts to you

Missed a run or ran long? The weeks ahead change to fit.

Your data, out

Export your runs as CSV or JSON, or as a summary you can hand to whoever coaches you.

Your data

No account and no network connection. Health data is read on your phone and stays there. Nothing is used for advertising, and nothing is shared unless you export a file yourself. The full version is in the privacy policy.
