Support

Help and contact. Last updated 13 October 2026. Rungud has not been released yet.

The quickest way to get help is to write to GudApps@icloud.com. I read everything and answer it myself.

Inside the app, Settings has a "Send feedback" row. It opens an email in your own mail app. You write it and you send it, and nothing is sent without you.

Common questions

My runs are not showing.
Rungud reads running and walking workouts from Apple Health. Open the Health app, tap your picture, then Apps, then Rungud, and check that workouts are allowed.

Does Rungud record runs?
No. An Apple Watch records them, and so does any app that saves running workouts to Apple Health. An iPhone on its own does not. Rungud reads what is in Apple Health.

How do I change or remove my plan?
Open Settings from the gear on Today and look under Plan.

Can it put a run on my Apple Watch?
Yes, if you have a paired Apple Watch. Rungud hands the run to Apple's Workout app, and it appears in the Workout app on your Watch. Settings has a page that walks through it.

How do I stop the reminders?
Turn off "Remind me on run days" in Settings, under Plan. Or turn off notifications for Rungud in the iPhone's Settings app.

Will my plan move to a new iPhone?
Only if the new phone is restored from a backup of the old one. The plan is a file on your iPhone and is kept nowhere else, because Rungud has no account and no server. Without a backup, make a new plan: your runs are still in Apple Health, and Rungud builds from them.

How do I delete my data?
Deleting the app removes your plan, your settings and the counts it keeps. Your workouts stay in Apple Health, which Rungud never changes.

Is this medical advice?
No. Rungud gives training guidance only. If you have any health concerns, talk to a doctor before you start a plan. If something hurts or feels wrong, stop and see someone.
