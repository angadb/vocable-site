Common questions

My runs are not showing.
Rungud reads running workouts from Apple Health. Check that it has permission in the Health app, under Sharing, Apps, Rungud.

Does Rungud record runs?
No. Record with your watch or any app that saves to Apple Health. Rungud reads what is there.

How do I change or remove my plan?
Open Settings from the gear on Today and look under Plan.

How do I delete my data?
Deleting the app removes your plan and settings. Your runs stay in Apple Health, which Rungud never changes.

Is this medical advice?
No. Talk to a doctor before starting a training plan if you have any health concerns.
