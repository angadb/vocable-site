Support

Help and contact

The quickest way to get help is to write to VocableSupport@icloud.com. I read everything and answer it myself.

Common questions

Does Vocable cost anything?
No. Every feature is free. There is an optional tip jar in Settings.

How do I move my words to a new iPhone?
Turn on iCloud sync in Settings on both devices, or export your data as a file from Settings.

A definition is missing or wrong.
Definitions come from the Free Dictionary API. Send me the word and I'll look into it.

How do I get a refund for a tip?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?
Deleting the app removes what is stored on the phone. If iCloud sync was on, you can also remove Vocable's data in Settings, under your name, iCloud, Manage Storage.
