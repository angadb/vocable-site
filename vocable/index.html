What it does

A word every morning

Its meaning, an example, and a button to hear it said. No repeats until you have seen them all.

Still with you?

An earlier word comes back with only its meaning. Know it and the gap grows: 2 days, then 7, then 30.

Ten seconds is enough

Opening the app counts. The streak has a weekly freeze, because life happens.

A quiz when you want one

Five words, no typing, no setup. Words you miss come back sooner.

Works offline

Every word the app ships with has its meaning built in.

Reminders you choose

The word in the morning, at the time you pick. Everything else stays quiet unless you turn it on.

Free, with a tip jar

There is no subscription and nothing to unlock. An optional tip inside the app helps cover the developer account fee.

Your data

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your words stay on your phone and in your own iCloud, where sync is on unless you turn it off in Settings. Meanings for the app's own words are built in. Looking up any other word asks the Free Dictionary API, which is sent that word and nothing else. The full version is in the privacy policy.
