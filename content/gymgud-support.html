Common questions

Do I need Rungud?
No. Gymgud plans your gym days around your runs, and the runs can come from Rungud, from three answers in setup, from a guess based on your Apple Health runs, or not at all.

Where do my runs come from?
Only from what you choose in setup or in Settings, under Runs. If you let it, Gymgud reads running workouts from Apple Health. To change that, open the Health app, tap your picture, then Apps, then Gymgud.

What does Gymgud write to Apple Health?
One strength workout for each session you finish, with its start and end time. No sets, no heart rate, no calories. If you say no when asked, it saves nothing.

I forgot to finish a session.
Gymgud finishes it for you an hour after your last set. You can also delete a session from its day in the Week tab.

Where is my lift log kept?
On your iPhone, with a restore copy in your own iCloud Drive. The copy is for a new phone or a reinstall, not live sync between devices. Settings, under Your data, says whether it is on.

How do I move to a new iPhone?
Sign in to the same iCloud account and open Gymgud. When it finds your copy and your log is empty, it asks whether to restore. Or export a backup from Settings, send the file to yourself, and import it on the new phone. Your week setup is not in the iCloud copy, so you answer the setup questions again.

Can I get my data out?
Yes. Settings, under Your data, can export a spreadsheet with one row per set, weights in kilograms, or a backup file with everything including photos.

Does it remind me to train or ring when the rest timer ends?
No. Gymgud sends no notifications. The rest timer is a countdown you see in the app.

How do I delete my data?
Deleting the app removes the log on your iPhone. The restore copy stays in your iCloud until you remove it: iPhone Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Storage, Gymgud. Your strength workouts stay in Apple Health until you delete them there, because Gymgud never deletes from Health. Dated safety copies are in the Files app, under On My iPhone, then Gymgud.

Is this medical advice?
No. Gymgud gives training guidance only. If you have any health concerns, talk to a doctor. If something hurts or feels wrong, stop and see someone.
