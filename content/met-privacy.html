Met ("the App") is developed by Angad Singh Bedi ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

The short version: I don't collect your data. The App has no account, no servers and makes no network connections. What you note about people stays on your iPhone.

Data That Stays on Your Device

The following is stored locally on your device and is never sent anywhere:

  • The people you add: names, notes, facts, dates and how you know them
  • The faces drawn for them and the descriptions you wrote
  • Your circles and your review history
  • Your app settings
  • Simple counts of how you use the App, such as cards reviewed and faces drawn

The App does not use iCloud sync. You can erase everything from Settings, and deleting the App deletes this data.

Information About Other People

The App holds what you choose to write about people you know. It stays on your iPhone, I never see it, and the App's widgets show at most a count, a first name and a face, never a note.

Microphone and Speech

The microphone is used only while you have tapped the microphone button. What you say is turned into text on your iPhone by Apple's speech recognition. The audio is not recorded or kept, and neither the audio nor the text is sent to me.

Apple Intelligence

On iPhones that support it, the App uses Apple Intelligence to sort what you say into a card, and Apple's Image Playground to draw a face from your description. Both are features of iOS that run on your iPhone. The App does not send your notes to any service of mine or to any third party. Without Apple Intelligence the App still works: you fill in the card yourself and faces are shown as initials.

Your Numbers and Feedback

The App counts a few things on your iPhone, such as days opened, cards reviewed and faces drawn, and shows them to you in Settings. They are counts only, never a name or a note, and the App has no way to send them.

If you choose "Send feedback", the App opens an email to me in your own mail app. If you leave "Include my numbers" on, the counts are added to that email, where you can read, change or delete them before you send it. If you send it, I receive your email address and whatever the email contains, and I use them only to read and answer your feedback.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use the App's Siri shortcuts, iOS passes what you say to the App on your device. Apple's own privacy policy covers Siri.

Network Requests, Analytics and Tracking

The App makes no network requests. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Apple gives app developers anonymous, combined statistics from people who have chosen to share them: how many people installed, opened or deleted the App, and crash reports. I can see those totals. I cannot see a person or a device. You control this on your iPhone in Settings, under Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements, Share With App Developers.

Children

The App collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
