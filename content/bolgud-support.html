Common questions

Why can't I see words yet?
A word appears only once you have met every letter in it. Letters come first, in lesson order, and the words follow.

How do the cards come back?
After each card you say Again, Hard, Good or Easy. The app uses that to decide when to show it next: cards that went well wait longer. Due cards come first each day, then new ones.

How many new cards do I get?
Five a day to start. Change it in Settings, from 1 to 20.

Can I turn off the Latin spelling?
Yes. Settings has a choice of a standard transliteration, a rough one, or none.

Is there audio?
Not yet. Exercises that need sound only appear when the sound is in the app.

I found a mistake.
The content has not yet been checked by a native speaker, so mistakes are possible. Please write to {{EMAIL}} and say which card.

How do I turn the reminder off?
Turn off the daily reminder in Settings, or turn off notifications for Bolgud in the iPhone's Settings app.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?
Only if the new phone is restored from a backup of the old one. Progress is kept on your iPhone and nowhere else, because Bolgud has no account and no server.

How do I delete my data?
Delete the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
