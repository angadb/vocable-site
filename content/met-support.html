Common questions

Minglegud didn't sort what I said.
Sorting needs Apple Intelligence, which needs a recent iPhone with it turned on in Settings. Without it you fill in the card yourself.

I can't draw a face.
Faces are drawn by Apple's Image Playground, which also needs Apple Intelligence. Otherwise use a photo or initials.

Does Minglegud read all my contacts?
No. It asks for access only when you tap "From Contacts", and copies only the people you pick.

Does anything I write about people leave my phone?
Not from Minglegud. There is no account, no sync and no network request of its own. Speech and drawing are done by Apple's features on your iPhone.

Does it work with Siri?
Not in this version.

How do I delete my data?
Use "Erase everything" in Settings, or delete the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
