Common questions

Does Vocable cost anything?
No. Every feature is free. There is an optional tip jar in Settings.

How do I move my words to a new iPhone?
Check that iCloud sync is on in Settings on both devices (it is on unless you turned it off), or export your data as a file from Settings.

A definition is missing or wrong.
The app ships with its own definitions. Words you look up yourself come from the Free Dictionary API. Send me the word and I'll look into it.

How do I get a refund for a tip?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?
Deleting the app removes what is stored on the phone. If iCloud sync was on (it is on unless you turned it off), you can also remove Vocable's data in Settings, under your name, iCloud, Manage Storage.
