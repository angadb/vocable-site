The quickest way to get help is to write to VocableSupport@icloud.com. I read everything and answer it myself.

Common questions

I don't know the exact date.
Choose "I'm not sure of the date" during setup. The app shows no phase until a start is logged.

How do I keep it private?
Turn on the app lock in Settings. Widgets and notifications never show dates or counts.

How do I move to a new iPhone?
Export a backup from Settings, send the file to yourself, and import it on the new phone.

How do I delete my data?
Deleting the app removes everything. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Is this medical advice?
No. CycleSync is not a medical device, a fertility tool or a method of contraception.
