What it does

One cue a day

A single suggestion for what to offer today. It never tells you how anyone feels.

A calendar that admits doubt

The next start is shown as a window, estimated from the dates you have logged.

A playbook from evidence

Short, practical and sourced. It includes what you need too.

Phases you can move

Change how long Comfort, Romance and Steady last. Patience stays counted back from the next start.

Discreet by design

Lock it with Face ID. Widgets, notifications and Siri never show dates or counts.

Without opening it

Widgets, a Lock Screen control and Shortcuts show the day's cue or log a new start.

Your data

No account, no analytics and no network requests of its own. The dates and settings you enter stay on your phone. A backup is a file you export yourself and keep wherever you like. The full version is in the privacy policy.
