CycleSync

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026. CycleSync has not been released yet.

CycleSync ("the App") is developed by Angad Singh Bedi ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

The short version: I don't collect your data. The App has no account, no servers and makes no network connections. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data That Stays on Your Device

The following is stored locally on your device and is never sent anywhere:

  • The cycle dates you log and the settings you choose
  • Your notification preferences
  • Whether the app lock is turned on

The App does not use iCloud sync. Deleting the App deletes this data.

Sensitive Information

Cycle dates are health-related information about another person. The App is built so that this information is not shown outside the App: its widgets and notifications never show dates or counts, and you can lock the App with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

App Lock

If you turn on the app lock, your iPhone checks your face, fingerprint or passcode. The App is only told whether the check passed. It never sees or stores biometric data.

Backups

You can export a backup file from Settings and import it again later. The file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to save or send it. I never receive it.

Your Numbers and Feedback

The App keeps a few plain counts of how you use it, on your iPhone only: the date you first opened it, how many days you opened it, how many times you opened the playbook, its basics page and the widget guide, how many times you exported or imported a backup, and whether you have logged a start, turned on the app lock or turned on reminders (yes or no only). These counts hold nothing about the cycle itself, such as dates, lengths or notes, and the App has no way to send them.

If you choose "Send feedback", the App opens an email to me in your own mail app. If you leave "Include in feedback emails" on, the counts are added to that email, where you can read, change or delete them before you send it. If you send it, I receive your email address and whatever the email contains, and I use them only to read and answer your feedback.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS. No notification service or server is involved.

Network Requests, Analytics and Tracking

The App makes no network requests. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Apple gives app developers anonymous, combined statistics from people who have chosen to share them: how many people installed, opened or deleted the App, and crash reports. I can see those totals. I cannot see a person or a device. You control this on your iPhone in Settings, under Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements, Share With App Developers.

Children

The App collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and its date.
