Gymgud.

Gymgud logs every set, shows whether each lift is moving, and says what to train today in plain words, with a day off legs before a hard run. It works alongside Rungud, or on its own.

What it does

Logs every set

Weight and reps, or seconds. An optional rest timer and one tap to undo a set.

Shows what is moving

Each lift is marked when it has stopped moving, with a chart of its last sessions.

A gym week around your runs

Runs come from Rungud, from three answers, or from your Apple Health runs. Or from nowhere: it works without them.

Saves to Apple Health

Each finished session is saved as a strength workout, with start and end time only.

Your data, out

A spreadsheet with a row per set, or a backup file with photos. Both go only where you send them.

Your data

No account, no analytics and no server of mine. Your lift log stays on your phone, with a restore copy in your own iCloud. It reads running workouts and body weight from Apple Health, and saves each session to it. If Rungud is installed, the two share your week through a folder on the phone. The full version is in the privacy policy.
