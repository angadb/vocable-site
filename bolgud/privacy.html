Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026. Bolgud has not been released yet.

Bolgud ("the App") is developed by Angad Singh Bedi ("I", "me"). This policy says what the App does with your data, and what it does not do.

The short version: I don't collect your data. The App has no account, no servers and makes no network requests. Your progress stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your progress on each card: when you last saw it, when it is next due, and how it went.
  • Your study sessions: the date, how many cards, and how long.
  • Your settings: new cards a day, how Gurmukhi is spelled out in Latin letters, and your reminder.
  • A one-week list for the widget (a letter or word, its name or meaning, and a short caption), kept in a storage space shared only between the App and its widget.

The letters, words and phrases you learn are built into the App. The App does not use iCloud and has no sync. Deleting the App deletes all of this.

Reminders

If you turn them on, the App sends one reminder a day at the time you pick. It is a local notification, made and scheduled on your iPhone, and it does not come from a server. It never says what you are learning. The App asks for permission only when you turn the reminder on.

Permissions it does not ask for

The App does not use your microphone, camera, photos, contacts, location or Apple Health. It does not record your voice or listen to you.

No account, no analytics, no server

There is no account. The App makes no requests to any server of mine, and I have none. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it has no feedback email or usage counts. The "Gud apps" row in Settings opens this website in your browser, which is your browser talking, not the App.

Apple gives app developers anonymous, combined statistics from people who have chosen to share them: how many people installed, opened or deleted the App, and crash reports. I can see those totals. I cannot see a person or a device. You control this on your iPhone in Settings, under Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements, Share With App Developers.

About the content

The letters, words and phrases have not yet been checked by a native speaker, and the App says so in Settings. If something looks wrong, it may be a mistake in the App.

Children

The App collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and its date.
