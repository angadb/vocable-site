What it does

Read the script

57 letter cards in lesson order, each with how it sounds and an example word.

Words unlock with letters

A word appears only once you have met every letter in it.

Cards come back on schedule

Say Again, Hard, Good or Easy and the card returns when you are due to forget it.

Then a spoken track

300 core words, plus phrases, verbs and folk sayings.

A letter a day on your Home Screen

An optional widget, and an optional daily reminder that never says what you are learning.

Your data

No account, no ads, no tracking and no network. Your progress stays on your phone. It does not use the microphone, camera or Health. The full version is in the privacy policy.
