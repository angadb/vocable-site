Support

Help and contact. Met has not been released yet.

The quickest way to get help is to write to VocableSupport@icloud.com. I read everything and answer it myself.

Common questions

Met didn't sort what I said.
Sorting needs Apple Intelligence, which needs a recent iPhone with it turned on in Settings. Without it you fill in the card yourself.

I can't draw a face.
Faces are drawn by Apple's Image Playground, which also needs Apple Intelligence. Otherwise Met shows initials.

Does anything I write about people leave my phone?
No. There is no account, no sync and no network connection.

How do I delete my data?
Use "Erase everything" in Settings, or delete the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
